## Phone Script (for clinics with no email or LinkedIn)

Best times to call: **8:55am** (before patients arrive) or **3:30 to 4pm** (afternoon lull). Avoid Mondays and lunch hour.

### When they pick up:

```
"Hi, I am [name] from Hilt Health. We make an AI powered
pre screening system for walk in clinics. Is [Dr. Name / the
clinic manager] available for about 2 minutes? I wanted to let
them know about a free trial we are offering Niagara clinics."
```

### If they put you through:

```
"Hi Dr. [Name], thanks for taking my call. I will be quick, I run
Hilt Health. We built a system where patients describe their symptoms
to an AI on a tablet while they wait, and you get a full summary
on his/her device before the examination starts. Saves you the first few minutes of every
appointment.

We are expanding into Niagara and offering clinics a free trial,
$200 worth of credits, no card required. Would it be worth 10
minutes to walk you through it? I can stop by the clinic or do
a quick Zoom."
```

### If the owner is not available (most likely):

```
"No worries. Could I get the best email to reach them? I will send
a quick note with a link to our site, it is hilthealth.com.
Takes 30 seconds to see what it does."
```

### If they will not give the email:

```
"Totally understand. Could you pass along my info? It is [name],
Hilt Health, and the website is hilthealth.com. We are offering a
free trial for Niagara clinics. They can reach me at
business@hilthealth.com."
```

### Receptionist tip:

Do not try to get past them, get them on your side. They experience the intake problem more than anyone. If they seem open:

```
"You probably see this every day, patients come in, wait, then
spend the first 5 minutes with the doctor explaining what is wrong.
We built something that handles that part while they wait.
Do you think [Dr. Name] would be open to hearing about it?"
```

**Remember:** The real goal of a phone call is to get the owner's email. That is the win even if you do not talk to them. Ask for their name too, "Sorry, I did not catch your name?", so you can reference them later.

## Rules

- **Personalize every message.** Their clinic name, their background, their city. Generic = deleted.
- **Always link to hilthealth.com.** Let the site do the heavy lifting on credibility.
- **"AI powered patient pre screening"**, use this phrase everywhere. Not "intake system," not "tablet system."
- **One follow up max.** If they do not respond twice, move to the next step (email, then phone, then in person).
- **Update the database.** After every touchpoint, update `outreach_step` and `status` in `clinic_prospects`.
- **Track in notes.** Date sent, response received, next action.
- **Focus on closing ONE clinic first.** Local social proof ("Dr. Bertolo at Glenridge is using this") changes everything.
